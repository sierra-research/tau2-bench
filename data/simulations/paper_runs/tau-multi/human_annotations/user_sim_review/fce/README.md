# First-critical-error and voice-experience review

`fce.csv` contains completed final reviews for 30 English calls and 30 calls in
each of the five localized paper languages. Within every language, it contains
10 calls from each domain and allocates systems as 8 OpenAI minimal, 7 OpenAI
xhigh, 8 Gemini minimal, and 7 Gemini high. The typed `domain` and `system`
columns make that balance machine-verifiable. The archive also retains task,
simulation, trial, final labels, scores, and notes.
